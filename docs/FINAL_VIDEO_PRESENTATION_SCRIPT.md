## 🎬 Section 3: Socratic Dialogue & Misconception DNA (1:30 – 2:30)

**[Visual Cue]**: Click **"Resume Socratic Tutor"** or navigate to **"Ask Neuro"** in the sidebar. Show the dialogue history.

**Speaker**:
> "Now let's step into **Ask Neuro**, the core tutoring experience.
> 
> Notice what happened when the student submitted:  
> *'I calculated the vertex for $f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3$ and got $x = -2$, because I used $x = b / (2a)$.'*
> 
> Rather than simply saying 'Wrong, the answer is 2', NeuroTeach:
> 1. **Identified the Misconception DNA**: `SIGN_ERROR_VERTEX` with 88% confidence.
> 2. **Selected a Pedagogical Strategy**: It selected **Counterexample** rather than giving away the computation.
> 3. **Delivered Socratic Guidance**: Prompting the learner to examine what happens to $-b$ when $b$ is already negative.
> 4. **Answer Leakage Filter**: Our deterministic SymPy engine filters out direct numeric leaks before serving the intervention."
